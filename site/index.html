HOW HIFZLY WORKS

Your routine has four parts:

  • New Hifz the Quran you are memorising now.
  • Recent Revision what you memorised lately, reinforced until it settles.
  • Old Revision older Hifz, so it is never neglected.
  • Tilawah / Musāfahah general recitation beyond your Hifz.

Set a daily target for each. Hifzly plans your week as six days and your month as four weeks, because life happens. Then record what you actually do and watch your consistency grow.
